Not sure if this is the right place to post but I genuinely don't know what to make of this.

So, I run a small freight operation on one of the many transit hubs in deep space. The hub is located between Terra and Mars, for reference. I do mostly short-distance runs, nothing exotic. Last week an order came in through the standard procurement platform. Anonymous client — that happens sometimes, usually corporates who don't want their name on smaller contracts. Fine.

But the order itself was strange. The items were a combination I've never seen together:
— Anti-radiation blocks. Industrial quantity. Twenty units.
— ARK-7 autonomous comms stations. Four of them.
— Dry rations. Ninety-day packs. Twenty sets.
— One sealed crate from a supplier I'd never dealt with before. The item description just said 'specialist equipment — ref. SE-FWD-01'. Do not open.

Payment was full upfront. Above standard rate. No questions asked on their end.

I fulfilled it. Pickup was done by two people, no names, correct confirmation code, gone in twenty minutes.

I'm not saying anything is wrong. But anti-radiation blocks in that quantity aren't something you order for a local trip. ARK-7 is autonomous comms — no external network needed. Ninety days of rations for twenty people.

Anyone seen an order like this before? Just trying to figure out what kind of operation that points to.

— Yael, Dross Freight Solutions
