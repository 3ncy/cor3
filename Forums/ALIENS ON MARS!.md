For decades, even before humans set foot on Mars by any official account, communications specialists have quietly acknowledged an unusual phenomenon affecting long-range transmissions originating from the planet. These are unexplained outbound signal contaminations.

This article is a response to new reports that have attributed the issue to atmospheric interference, geological activity, communication infrastructure, and simple transmission errors. I, like many independent researchers, argue that these explanations all fail to account for one crucial detail: the regularity and apparent structure of the interference.

Whether this points to an unknown natural phenomenon, classified technology, or something far stranger and beyond our comprehension is yet to be determined. However, if we stop looking, we will never know. Or, if it is already known, then the secret of it may be the planet’s biggest mystery, and may explain all the tension and the real reasons behind the fighting.

## Background

The reports of unusual radio emissions only started being called that after permanent settlements were formed, but that does not mean this is when they started. That could easily be dismissed as a human-caused issue. No, these inconsistencies have always been there.

Many uncovered reports about the planet from just before colonial efforts began indicate readings that were inconsistent and did not make sense when it came to radio signals and emissions coming from the planet. Even before colonisation efforts, and before the jump into space colonisation in general, research into Mars was always yielding results that were dismissed by scientists as “errors” and “outlier” readings. But mistakes do not follow the same pattern for so many years.

“If you take all the errors and outlier readings,” says an independent researcher who wishes to stay anonymous for the safety of his family, “you notice that they all follow the same period, one that happens to line up with how long it takes the Martian comms array to reset.”

This observation has been foolishly dismissed by those arguing that any random set of data over a long period can appear to create a pattern, but this is a misrepresentation of that scientific fact.

Skipping forward, reports of unusual transmissions started to appear shortly after permanent settlements were established on Mars. Communication engineers described intermittent bursts of unidentified electromagnetic activity that would interrupt outgoing transmissions and then disappear. These were again foolishly dismissed as mistakes, this time as hardware faults.

Archived maintenance bulletins recovered from civilian relay stations show technicians repeatedly documenting the same thing again and again and again over several decades, even after complete replacements of communications infrastructure over time. So if the equipment changed, why did the errors not change? And why is no one considering that it is more than an error? Unless they already know, of course.

## More than just static

What is very interesting, very, very interesting, is that if you actually look at the issue, not just the numbers but the actual issue, it becomes evident that the emissions display characteristics inconsistent with random interference and more like intentional communication. Like a bird calling and calling and calling.

This is very difficult to find, as most reports censor the actual features and only record superficial information, making the real data difficult to see. A major breakthrough in independent investigation happened when access to the settlements was briefly available to all and the signals could be experienced first-hand.

“The easiest explanation is equipment,” says one of the independent investigators who recorded his own data, causing him to get blacklisted and go into hiding, “but that would not be the same song for thirty years. What I found was a message coming out, or at least a signal. If not for someone, then from something we were definitely overhearing.”

But before he was able to decode it, his materials were confiscated.

“I would have found out soon, I am sure, and that is why I was silenced. I was almost there.”

CORE Communications has repeatedly stated that no evidence exists to suggest intentional transmissions, and has maintained that no confiscation or censorship related to the topic is in place. But what they call “welcoming independent investigation” suspiciously rarely yields results, with many independent investigators spending years and investing their lives into their work only to be forced to quit suddenly.

## The cover job

Modern communication networks automatically reconstruct damaged transmissions before they reach civilian users. The process is entirely routine and intended to improve communication reliability, but it progressed rapidly after connection to Mars increased and independent research about the transmissions started gaining traction.

Critics argue that this supposedly helpful technology creates an unexpected problem. If raw transmissions are automatically corrected before the public receives them, independent researchers have no way to determine what information, if any, is being discarded during reconstruction. Describing automated filtering as an industry standard necessary for interplanetary communication is a common response. However, if this is the case, then why is there no way for users to access the original files and raw information from the source if they wish?

## The “Underground Origin” theory

One of the most controversial claims, even within the community of speculators, comes from amateur radio operators. Despite the speculative nature of the claim, it is always worth exploring possible explanations, as the truth always sounds strange if it has never been heard before.

Several separate and independent groups have published analyses suggesting that the unidentified emissions consistently originate from fixed locations on the Martian surface rather than from orbital infrastructure.

I am not at liberty to share the location, for the safety of the official associated investigators carrying out further research, but all reports indicate a similar spot within a three-kilometre radius.

Some theorists venture even further. Based on estimated signal arrival angles, they argue that the source may originate beneath the surface itself. Professional researchers have criticised these findings, citing the limitations of civilian equipment and the difficulty of accurately locating long-range radio sources.

## What could be there

The mystery has become intertwined with persistent rumours regarding underground infrastructure on the Martian surface. These have been proposed as possible explanations for how some communities on the planet are able to navigate in ways that seem impossible to explain.

These networks could be naturally occurring lava tubes, as they are called. However, there may be a possibility that the communications are arriving from some place where something is either calling out, or where we are overhearing it.

## Classified or lost

One recurring point concerns the apparent absence of complete raw communication archives and access to raw communication information today. Officials insist that missing records are due to reasons ranging from bad record keeping, lost files, terror attacks and natural disasters. The lack of access to current raw data remains without comment.

Skeptics remain unconvinced by such responses.

## Could it be something else entirely?

This publication contacted several planetary scientists regarding the possibility that the signals represent extraterrestrial intelligence. None wanted to comment on the matter, possibly out of fear.

It is widely known that there is something unexplainable on the planet. Something people are fighting about, and maybe even something influencing decisions.

It is not an impossible idea that some groups on the planet found the source of this inconsistency, and maybe that is why the planet was shut off completely. It would explain why some people can operate differently, and so well. It would also explain why there is so much tension.

When we talk about this alien life, we are not yet sure what it means. There is some intelligence being tapped into. Something is happening.
