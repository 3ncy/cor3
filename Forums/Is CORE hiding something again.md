I was looking for old episodes of Good Morning, Sector Kids yesterday. Yeah, that dumb little kids' show with the singing animals. The one about order, safety, listening to adults, being a good little sector child, all that sweet harmless garbage. And guess what? A huge chunk of it is gone. Just gone. Especially the 2213–2219 run. Funny, right? The exact years they played that crap in every school, every kindergarten, every morning block before class.

The safe episodes are still there, of course. Bunny counts apples. Little girl washes her hands. Cute music, bright colors, nothing to see. But the ones with the blue bird? The ones where it sings, If you're lost, call an elder, Don't argue with elders, The elder knows the way? Gone. All of them. Wiped clean. But sure, old files, dead links, nobody cares, server space, whatever. Keep swallowing that.

Do you people still think those were cartoons? They were training tapes. Corporate obedience dressed up as children's songs. No chips, no injections, no secret labs needed. Then the kid grows up. Gets a desk job. Gets a terminal. Gets a dispatch chair. Gets admin access. And when CORE does another filthy thing, he shrugs and says: Well, who else is going to keep order?

That's it. That's the trick.

That's why they're cleaning those episodes now. People started comparing the lyrics to current staff instructions, and it's not kind of similar. Some of it is word for word. But go on, laugh.
