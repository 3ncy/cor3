It started spreading through old systems like it knew where to go.

So tell me why everyone is so sure it stayed stupid.

Around heat, pressure, chemical waste, dead tissue, human waste, old control circuits, signal lines, sensor grids, and closed air loops. You let it sit there long enough to learn every little rhythm of the system. You think it stays "just mold" because some corporate scientist wrote that on a label? No. It becomes part of the system. Then it becomes the system.

That is where the blue mold comes in.
