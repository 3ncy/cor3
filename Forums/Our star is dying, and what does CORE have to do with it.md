Let's start simple: a star is not a light bulb. It is a massive thermonuclear reactor. Inside the Sun, hydrogen turns into helium, energy is released, and pressure keeps the star from collapsing. All of it depends on balance. Temperature, mass, magnetic field, radiation, plasma flows, solar wind. Any normal astrophysicist will tell you: a star lives because of equilibrium.

Now here is the question. What happens if someone starts draining energy from it on an industrial scale?

And I am not talking about the panels on the roof of your residential block. I am talking about CORE. About their "solar maintenance grid" project. The one no ordinary person has ever visited and never will. They tell us nothing about this project. Through that infrastructure, they are draining our star dry. Yes. Exactly that.

Have you seen the solar constant graphs? There are drops. Small ones, yes, but regular. And if you actually look, there is a pattern. Drain, pause, drain, pause, drain again. Like someone is slowly, step by step, pulling the star's own energy out of it.

And now the worst part. They are not stealing that energy for stations. And not for us at all.

They are charging a huge ship.

And it is not a normal ship. It is an ark. A closed evacuation project for the top of CORE and the rest of the elite. For the people they consider worthy of a new civilization. Yes, exactly. A new civilization. Because they do not need the old one anymore.

They have known for a long time that the Sun will not survive. More precisely, it is not the Sun that will fail on its own. They will finish it off with their energy drain. They need to charge new, ultra-secret, high-capacity superconducting NIC cores, zero-phase accumulators, magnetic transition stabilizers, and that whole closed nullT system. In plain human language: they are building a wormhole.

The main question is: where to?

TRAPPIST-1e.

Look it up yourself. A real planet. About the size of Earth. Located in a zone where liquid water could theoretically exist. Orbiting the red dwarf TRAPPIST-1.

Now ask yourself what happens to everyone else. To those who did not get a ticket to the ark.

Without the Sun, we all die. But they do not need the whole human anthill. They do not need workers from orbital stations. They do not need colonists, the sick, the old, the poor. They need a small, clean version of humanity that they can take to a new planet and say: now we start properly.

And we will be left here. In a system with a dying star.

That is why CORE is not helping to save Earth. Because they were never planning to save it. They have already found themselves a new, clean, unspoiled Earth where they can live like they are in paradise.

And the rest of us will just be thrown away.

Like a used condom.
