Date: 15.05.2251

Priority Level: STANDARD

Distribution: AVEN Supply Marketplace — System Administrators

Subject: Purchase Quantity Limitation — Functionality Testing

**Order Summary**

By this directive, AVEN Supply marketplace administrators are instructed to conduct functionality testing of purchase quantity limitations. The testing shall verify correct restriction behaviour for different user tiers.

**Scope of Testing**

Administrators are to simulate transaction scenarios for each user classification and confirm that quantity restrictions are enforced according to the current specification. Any deviation, bypass, or inconsistency in limit application shall be logged and reported immediately.

**Compliance**

This order shall remain in effect until testing is complete and results are verified. Deviation from protocol requires prior authorisation from Council oversight.
