### hoofman_notes.doc

```text
personal notes / not for formal filing

The biggest mistake is the assumption these people started with a religion, or even the name
Cult at all.

    This didn't start as a religion.
    This started as an abandoned and starving people.

ORIGINS:
        Most accounts trace the movement to survivors of failed early colonial expeditions,
        namely Mars Uno. Recurring themes: abandonment, starvation, isolation, distrust of
        external authority.

    I believe this narrative is inaccurate, but believed.
    The history is not as important as the fact people believe it.

NEW TECHNICIANS:
        Observed duties: route selection, environmental assessment, resource allocation,
      - settlement planning, medical recommendations.

        They are the knowledge bearers.

        Incident: A civilian convoy altered course through a canyon through consultation with a NT,
        adding three days to journey. Original route later became impassable following a collapse.

        Fred went to interview the group but has not yet returned.
        This is concerning.

        Working theory: either the NTs receive information beforehand through some surveying
        method, or… what else could it be?

SHEPARDS:
        Community management, public speaking, ritual leadership, resource distribution.
        They relay what the NTs provide.

        I have attended some Sermons. Unsure about reporting this back.
        I don't believe it counts as infiltration - the Sermons are broadcast remotely through
        speakers throughout civilian areas.

CONCERNS:
        When Mike was exploring a Cult settlement miles away, their information aligned with
        predictions from the main target settlement. Either a secret communication network exists,
        or some form of written records or signals.

PCF RELATIONSHIP:
        If there was a strong link before, it has substantially weakened now. They collaborate
        but remain separate organisations.

        Recon on PCF has come to a halt since Peters was compromised and killed. Perpetrator unknown.

OUTSTANDING QUESTIONS:
        How do NTs acquire information?
        Who are the decision makers?
        Where is MP?
        And why can I not infiltrate….

WORKING IDEA:
        It would be a huge intelligence failure to dismiss them as irrational, and an analytical
        failure to assume they even understand their own success.
```
