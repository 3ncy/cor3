No immediate family is recorded, no wife or kids. No information about any significant other as well. The guy is keeping his stuff to himself.

His colleagues say he plays poker with them from time to time but mostly keeps things professional. Chris has been spotted playing virtual games on several occasions (or at least using a VR headset) but no further details are known.
