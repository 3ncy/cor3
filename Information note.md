## Purpose of the COR3.GG

Enter a dedicated environment where you become part of the Fragmentary Order quickly and go through a full stage of progression within it — with the opportunity to be among the first.

Step into a world where you don't just observe, but actively shape your presence: gain experience, build connections, develop your own system, and make decisions that define your path forward.

Over time, you establish your foundation — knowledge, reputation, resources, tools, and capabilities — that become your real asset.

Everything you build in COR3.GG — your knowledge, experience, connections, system, and accumulated resources — can be transferred into Fragmentary Order and directly impact your starting position in the main game.


## <img src="Misc assets/BrainIcon.svg"/> Hardcore Mode 

Optional content

By selecting this mode, you begin your character's journey from the very start.


Discover how it all began, meet key figures, and explore the world behind the main experience.

This mode features complex challenges that require strong analytical thinking and attention to detail.

It is entirely optional, but completing these missions grants the following rewards in Deployment Mode:
* Unique achievements
* Credit rewards
* Guaranteed [Pathfinder rank](Ranks.md#pathfinder) in Deployment Mode

⚠ The highest rank obtainable through Hardcore Mode is Pathfinder.

You can skip Hardcore Mode at any time using the `Restore` command in the terminal and switch to Deployment Mode. Restoring your account will reset all progress and start your journey from the beginning.
![Termial screen](Misc%20assets/terminal-restore.png)

## <img src="Misc assets/DeploymentHexIcon.svg"/> Deployment Mode

Main content

By selecting this mode, you enter the main phase of the experience.

The early story is skipped, allowing you to focus on progression, operations, and expansion of your system. This is the default mode, where your account begins standing and advancement.

Here you will:
* Develop your account and participate in active operations
* Build your system — resources, connections, tools, and capabilities — to transfer into Fragmentary Order
* Focus on the mid program and compete for higher ranks based on your performance. Progress through operations and earn reputation, influence, and overall standing.
* If you skip Hardcore Mode and proceed here, your journey will begin at the [Foundation rank](Ranks.md#foundation).

Access to earlier Acts is not available in this mode.
