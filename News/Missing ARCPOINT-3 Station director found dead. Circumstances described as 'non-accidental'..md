# Missing ARCPOINT-3 Station director found dead. Circumstances described as 'non-accidental'.

| Source | | Date | Author |
|-|-|-|-|
| ![](news/snn_source_icon.png) | SNN | 2251-07-19 | SNN Staff · CRIME & SECURITY |

![](news/elias_vorn_official_portrait.jpg)

Elias Vorn, 54, director of ARCPOINT-3 orbital station, was reported missing eighteen days ago after failing to appear for a scheduled board meeting. Station security initially classified the case as an unexplained absence.

His body was discovered yesterday in a sealed industrial container in the station's lower storage section during a routine inventory check. The ARCPOINT-3 Station Security Service, which is leading the investigation, confirmed that the circumstances of death are being treated as non-accidental. No further details were provided.

Vorn had served as station director for eleven years. Prior to his appointment he held senior administrative roles at two other orbital facilities.

'We are fully cooperating with the investigation and have placed all relevant station records at the disposal of investigators.' — ARCPOINT-3 Station public affairs office.

No arrests have been made. The investigation is ongoing.
