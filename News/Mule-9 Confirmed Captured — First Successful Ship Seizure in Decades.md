# "Mule-9" Confirmed Captured — First Successful Ship Seizure in Decades

The Port Authority of REPNODE M7 has officially confirmed what rumour circuits have been whispering for the last two days: the cargo ship Mule-9 did not arrive at its scheduled berth. It is gone.

With this confirmation, speculation hardens into fact. The vessel has been taken. Only its escape pods remain — jettisoned, scattered, and carrying the shaken remnants of its crew.

Rescue operations are ongoing. Sources close to the effort report that not all Mule-9 personnel have been accounted for. Search patterns continue across the last known operational zone, but time is against the recovery teams. Some crew members are in no condition to speak. Others have spoken — and their accounts do not align.

What is known is this: a fully modernised cargo ship, equipped with current-generation security and monitoring systems, simply lost communications. When an investigation group reached its last reported coordinates, there was no wreckage, no debris field. Only the faint, looping distress signals of escape pods drifting in the black.

This represents the first successful capture of a spacefaring vessel in decades. It may also be one of the most flawlessly executed operations ever recorded. No security protocol triggered. No distress call was received. The ship was there — and then it was not.

The unanswered questions are stacking high. Who has the capability to execute such an operation? What was Mule-9 carrying? And what response will come from COR3, whose jurisdiction this falls squarely within?

Officially, silence. Unofficially, a hundred theories are already moving through the dark.

SNN will continue to search. And we will keep you updated.
