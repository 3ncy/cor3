# Marcus Vale still has not made contact after "72 hours without CORIE" challenge

More than 72 hours have passed since Marcus Vale began his "72 hours without CORIE" challenge, but the influencer still has not made contact.

Marcus, a CEDRT employee also known for his lifestyle experiments and tech challenges, announced the start of the trial earlier this week. The rules were simple: three days without CORIE's assistance.

After the deadline passed, Marcus still did not appear.

Friends and colleagues have not commented on the situation. The influencer's team released a short statement saying they are "aware of the concern surrounding Marcus Vale" and are "trying to establish contact with him." At the time of publication, no official emergency notice had been issued.

The challenge was supposed to show that a person can still live without CORIE.

So far, it shows exactly the opposite.
