# USOL residential rates: why prices are rising everywhere at once

| Source | | Date | Author |
|-|-|-|-|
| ![](news/snn_source_icon.png) | SNN | 2251-07-01 | Vera Komarek · Investigative

![](news/usol_housing_collage.jpg)

Over the past eight months, rental rates for residential modules across all USOL-managed orbital facilities have increased by an average of 67 to 89 percent. SNN reviewed data from seventeen stations. The pattern is identical across all of them.

This is unusual. The orbital rental market has historically shown local dynamics — prices on each station moved independently, driven by local migration flows, production cycles, and infrastructure events. A synchronized increase across all facilities of a single operator is rare.

USOL's official position: population growth. A statement from the faction representative reads: 'Rental rates reflect the real balance of supply and demand at each facility.' The data does not match.

![USOL residential rental rates — 18-month comparison. Source: Orbital Property Registry (open data).](https://cdn.cor3.gg/corie/news/usol_rent_infographic.jpg)

'Look at these curves. They're parallel. That means the decision was made centrally and simultaneously. That's not what happens when you respond to a market. That's what happens when you create one.' — independent housing market analyst, name withheld.

Residents of USOL facilities have reported not only rising prices. SNN received messages from multiple stations describing a consistent pattern: recurring technical failures in residential blocks, lengthy delays from maintenance services, and offers to relocate to 'enhanced comfort modules' at significantly higher rates.

'Our ventilation has been under planned maintenance for three weeks. Every time we call, they say a little longer. Two neighbors moved to the comfort blocks. Their old units are still empty.' — resident of a USOL facility, name withheld.

SNN sent requests to USOL and the orbital housing regulator. USOL did not respond within the stated timeframe. The regulator confirmed receipt of the inquiry. This investigation is ongoing.
