# Band from Titan releases album made from the sounds of methane storms

| Source | | Date | Author |
|-|-|-|-|

![](news/burnssy_rain.jpg)

"Pale Weather", a band based on Titan, has released a new album built from processed recordings of methane storms and the low-frequency hum of the external structures of a dome settlement.

The album is titled Burnssy Rain. According to the musicians, part of the material was recorded during a seasonal storm over the northern methane sea, when the wind battered the station's external masts for several hours and sensors recorded wind speeds of around 91 meters per second.

Technically, these are not storm songs in their raw form. The source data went through audio conversion, stretching, filtering, and synthesis. The result is slow industrial ambient with deep bass, metallic overtones, and a rhythm that sounds more like a huge machine at work than ordinary music.

Critics are already calling the album "the coldest release of the year." The band describes it more simply: "This is not music about Titan. This is music from Titan itself."
