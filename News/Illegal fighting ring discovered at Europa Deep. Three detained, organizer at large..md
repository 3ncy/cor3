# Illegal fighting ring discovered at Europa Deep. Three detained, organizer at large.

| | | |
|-|-|-|
| ![](news/snn_source_icon.png) | SNN | 2251-07-14 |

![](news/europa_deep_official.jpg)

According to a statement from Europa Deep Mining Consortium, the venue was discovered in sector E-7 of the lower horizon during a routine inspection. Preliminary data suggests fights were held on a regular basis over a period of several years.

Three facility employees have been detained and charged with participation in unauthorized activity and breach of employment contract. The identities of those detained are not being disclosed. The organizer of the events has not been identified and is believed to have left the facility shortly before the venue was discovered.

'This kind of thing is a consequence of the conditions at isolated facilities. People are in an enclosed space for months. No real recreational options, no way to leave. We don't condone it, but we understand why it happens.' — source within the Europa Deep administration, name withheld.

Europa Deep Mining Consortium stated it will implement additional recreational provisions for employees and increase patrols of lower horizons.

This is the third such incident in the past two years at isolated mining facilities in the outer sectors. In both previous cases, the organizers were also never identified.
