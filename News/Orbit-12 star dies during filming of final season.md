# Orbit-12 star dies during filming of final season

|-|-|-|-|

Production on the final season of Orbit-12 has been suspended after actress Selene Arkwright suddenly died on set.

Arkwright was best known for her role as Commander Lyra Sane and had been one of the main faces of the series since its first season. For many fans, her character was the emotional center of Orbit-12. Not the loudest, not the flashiest, but the one who held the human side of the story together.

According to the studio's statement, the actress suffered an acute medical episode during a filming day. Emergency services were called to the set, but she could not be saved. No further details about the cause of death have been released.

The final season of Orbit-12 had already been under close fan scrutiny. Production had been delayed several times, rumors about a rewritten ending had leaked online, and some viewers were convinced that the studio was trying to finish the series faster than the team was actually ready for.

After news of Arkwright's death broke, actors, directors, and fans began posting condolences. The series director wrote that "Selene was the person who made Orbit feel truly alive." Some of her colleagues on set limited their response to a black screen and short requests not to disturb the family.

Holoscene Interactive, one of the series' key production partners, also released a brief statement expressing condolences to Arkwright's family, colleagues, and fans.

It is still unclear whether the final season will be completed, rewritten, or delayed indefinitely. Official promotion for Orbit-12 has already been put on hold.

Fans, of course, have already started building theories and looking for hidden reasons. But for now, only one fact has been confirmed: one of the most recognizable actresses of her generation is gone just before the finale of her defining role.
