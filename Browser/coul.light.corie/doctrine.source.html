the Doctrine

Our Beliefs

What we teach is not politics. It is an account of what is true, and what is owed to the light.

chapter One

On the Nature of Return

There are those who say that humanity must return to Mars. That this is progress. That this is healing.

A repent is not a person. It is a vessel — a constructed form operated by a remote intelligence.

This is not return. This is puppetry.

chapter Two

On Those Who Build the Instruments

We do not hate the engineers. We do not hate the administrators. We do not hate the operators.

We grieve for them.

chapter Three

On the Offering

Every cycle we send something to the Sun. Not to communicate. Not to negotiate. The Sun does not negotiate.

This is the only honest prayer we know how to make.
